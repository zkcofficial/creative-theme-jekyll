About Shoshana Cox

Shoshana Cox is an AI security architect, researcher, and strategist with over a decade of experience in mission-critical AI. Her work focuses on bridging the gap between probabilistic systems and deterministic security requirements, and providing high-level strategy and training for C-suite executives and technical teams globally. Shoshana's 2022 & 2025 papers on adversarial AI attack & defense were validated by a NIST-published mathematical proof & resulting recommendations, representing a rare post-hoc mathematical confirmation of engineering work. Currently serving as the CEO and Head of Research at Bermuda Hundred Strategies, she specializes in the intersection of AI, national security, and threat modeling. Shoshana is a member of the core author team for the OWASP AI Exchange, where she contributes to international engineering standards and technical requirements for the EU AI Act. Her professional background spans roles as a mathematician, red team lead, data scientist, and Chief Data Officer. Shoshana also serves as an AI threat modeling subject matter expert and instructor. Shoshana is widely recognized for her work on AI red teaming, MLSecOps and AI defensive architectures, authoring technical papers and holding a patent in AI security (US 12,093,400 B1). She is an active voice in the industry through her Substack newsletter Angles of Attack, where she provides in-depth analysis on topics like agentic threat modeling, AI red teaming, and the security risks of AI.
Some highlights of Shoshana’s published works: “Securing AIML Systems in the Age of Information Warfare”, field’s first MLSecOps paper in 2022 prior to ChatGPT “Quantifying The Risk Of Transferred Black Box Attacks” in 2025, mathematically demonstrating AI red teaming was invalid for security US Patent US12093400B1, “Systems and methods for model security in distributed model training applications” Shoshana’s core work on AI security was validated by NIST (The National Institute of Standards and Technology) mathematical proof in 2026.
